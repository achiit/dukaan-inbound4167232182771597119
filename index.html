Krishna Kant

Owner, Wempire

In 2020 I opened Wempire with a single rack and a small loan from my elder brother. I was tired of seeing friends travel all the way to Gaya town just to buy one good shirt.

Six years later, boys from Dobhi come in for their first interview shirt, and some bring their fathers for wedding season. That is the best part of this work.

I only sell men's wear, so I know every size, fit and fabric on my shelf. If something does not suit you, I will tell you.
